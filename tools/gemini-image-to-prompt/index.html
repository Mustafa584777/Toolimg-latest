Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI Image to Prompt generator work? ▼

The tool employs advanced Gemini vision capabilities to meticulously analyze the color matrix, medium, geometry, lighting, subject matter, and composition setup of your uploaded image. It then reverse-engineers specific descriptive parameters tailored exactly to standard generator engines.

Which AI image generators are supported? ▼

We generate tailored prompt parameters for Midjourney v6 (including aspect ratios, styling raw parameters, chaos levels), Stable Diffusion XL (using keyword tag formats), DALL-E 3 (descriptive natural logic), FLUX.1 (modern instruction-rich layouts), and highly photorealistic specs.

Is my uploaded image saved or stored privately? ▼

Yes, your images are parsed in memory and analyzed over secure, encrypted channels. We do not store or use your uploaded imagery for general model training or index them in search archives. Your history of generated text is safely saved in local storage on your device.
