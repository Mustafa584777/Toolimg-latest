Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI Image to Prompt generator work? ▼

The tool employs advanced Gemini vision capabilities to meticulously analyze the color matrix, medium, geometry, lighting, subject matter, and composition setup of your uploaded image. It then reverse-engineers specific descriptive parameters tailored exactly to standard generator engines.

Which AI image generators are supported? ▼

We generate tailored prompt parameters for Midjourney v6 (including aspect ratios, styling raw parameters, stylize levels), Stable Diffusion XL (using keyword tag formats), DALL-E 3 (descriptive natural logic), FLUX.1 (modern instruction-rich layouts), and highly photorealistic specs.

How are credits deducted? ▼

Each reverse engineering extraction consumes 2 credits. Guests receive starter credits, and logged-in users synchronize credits live with their ToolIMG account balance.

Is my uploaded image saved or stored privately? ▼

Yes, images are processed securely in memory and never used to train public models. Your reverse engineered history is conveniently stored in your local browser storage.
